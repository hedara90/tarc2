# Pokémon Recordkeepers
A roguelite romhack of Pokemon Emerald.

Play as Tiana, a new Recordkeeper exploring simulations of the fall of ancient legends.

Each delve into the old legends will be different as you overcome obstacles and grow stronger on the path to the final legend and witness the conclusion yourself.

Build combinations of abilities and moves never seen before as in order to prevail, or fail and try again.

Features:

- New unique asymmetrical battle system

- Unique boss and player mechanics

- Up to 4 abilities per Pokémon

- 80+ new abilities

- 14 new moves

- New maps with all new graphics

- Completely new UI's for almost everything
